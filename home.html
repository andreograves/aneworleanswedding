Getting Here

Fly into Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport (MSY), about 25 minutes from the French Quarter by car.

Getting Around

The Capulet is in the Bywater, about ten blocks downriver from the French Quarter. The French Quarter, Marigny and Garden District are best explored on foot or by streetcar, and rideshares are easy to find throughout the city.
